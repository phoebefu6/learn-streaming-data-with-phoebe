Learn Streaming Data with Phoebe

At 2pm the revenue dashboard still shows midnight, because the pipeline runs nightly. This course closes that 14-hour gap the honest way: flip the stream on and watch the number go WRONG, then earn correctness back one lever at a time - idempotency, event-time windows, watermarks, replay. Every number in the simulator is computed live from a deterministic day of coffee-shop orders. And the first lesson is the unfashionable one: most dashboards should stay batch.

The six sessions ⚡ · one dashboard, upgraded across all of them

The running artifact is Daybreak, the coffee shop from Learn Data Pipelines, now demanding its numbers in real time. Same orders, same world - this is that course's real-time sibling.

🕑 Session 1 · easy

The stale dashboard

The nightly batch is not wrong, it is old. What an event is, why facts beat rows, and the three-zone test for whether you need streaming at all.

▶ Start here 📜 Session 2 · medium

The log

Topics, partitions, offsets and consumer groups - the four ideas under every streaming system. Flip the stream on and watch freshness arrive and honesty leave.

Kafka core 🔁 Session 3 · medium

In and out

At-least-once is the default, retries arrive twice, and exactly-once is narrower than the brochure says. Idempotency fixes the double-count for real.

halfway scorecard 🪟 Session 4 · hands-on

Windowing

Tumbling, hopping and session windows, and the two clocks. Doing it right makes the number visibly WORSE - the most honest dip in the course.

the dip ⏳ Session 5 · hands-on

Late data

Watermarks decide how long an hour stays open. Stragglers get caught, the truly late get counted instead of vanishing, and replay converges the day to 100%.

watermarks + replay 🚢 Session 6 · hardest

Ship it

Consumer lag as the one metric that matters, what always-on infra really bills, lambda vs kappa from the people who coined them - and the lever that never helps.
